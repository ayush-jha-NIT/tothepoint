## What is Vastu?

*Vastu Shastra* refers to traditional Indian ideas associated with buildings, layout and orientation. Modern consultations may discuss how rooms, entrances and living areas are positioned, using principles from that tradition.

Vastu is a cultural and interpretive framework. It is separate from the engineering and safety rules that apply to actual buildings, and it does not provide scientific guarantees of prosperity or health.

## Common topics in a consultation

People often raise questions about a main entrance, a workspace, the arrangement of rooms or the use of an existing property.

When discussing these topics, distinguish between **traditional symbolism** and practical concerns. Lighting, ventilation, safe access and structural conditions should be evaluated with appropriate professional advice.

A change to room arrangements may involve ordinary design preferences, but altering a wall or electrical installation can have real safety implications.

## Separating tradition from safety

A building's structural integrity, emergency exits, sanitation and compliance with local regulations cannot be assessed through astrology. Where a proposal might affect these matters, use a qualified architect, engineer or relevant authority.

Similarly, health symptoms should not be attributed to the orientation of a room without appropriate medical assessment.

Vastu interpretations should never override these responsibilities.
