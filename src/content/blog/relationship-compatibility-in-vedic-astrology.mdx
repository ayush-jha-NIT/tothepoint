## What does compatibility mean?

In everyday life, compatibility can describe shared values, mutual respect, communication and practical expectations. In Vedic astrology, the same word can refer to traditional methods used to compare birth charts.

These are different kinds of assessment. Chart interpretation is a cultural or belief-based practice; it cannot replace getting to know someone or discussing important decisions together.

## What traditional matching considers

Some forms of Vedic marriage matching consider chart-based classifications and symbolic relationships between placements. You may hear terms such as *Guna Milan* or *Kundli matching*. The specific method and its emphasis can vary.

If you are seeking a comparison, ask which information is required for both people. Accurate birth details may affect traditional calculations, but incomplete information should be disclosed rather than guessed.

A responsible discussion explains the system being used without presenting numerical matching results as a scientific measure of relationship success.

## What a chart cannot tell you

No chart can establish another person's consent, honesty, intentions or willingness to build a relationship. Those matters require real-world communication, boundaries and observation.

Likewise, an astrological interpretation cannot guarantee a marriage date, prevent conflict or determine whether two people should remain together. Serious relationship distress, abuse or mental-health concerns require appropriate support beyond astrology.

## Preparing your questions

A useful enquiry starts with the specific concern: marriage planning, compatibility traditions, communication questions or family expectations.

Consider asking:

- Which matching method is being discussed, and what does it mean?
- Is another person's birth information necessary?
- What are the limits of the analysis?
- What fees, session format and duration apply?
