## What is numerology?

Numerology is a collection of traditions that attach symbolic meaning to numbers. A practitioner may calculate values using a date of birth or the letters in a name, then discuss interpretations associated with those values.

Numerology is **not a scientifically validated predictive method**. It can be approached as cultural symbolism or a reflective exercise, but not as proof about a person's future, health or financial outcomes.

## More than one method

There is no single numerology method used by everyone. Systems may differ in how they assign numbers to letters, combine digits or interpret the result.

This is why the first question in a numerology consultation should be which system is being used. Two people using different systems can reach different calculations without either being a universal standard.

It is also useful to ask whether the interpretation depends on a full name, a chosen name, a birth date or another piece of information.

## Reading symbolic meanings

Numerology discussions sometimes include terms such as *birth number*, *life path number* or *name number*. These terms describe calculations and the meanings assigned to them inside a tradition.

A number by itself does not guarantee luck, success, compatibility or a particular personality. Claims that a number can cure a medical condition or secure financial returns should be treated critically.

You can still ask how an interpretation is constructed and what it means to the practitioner.

## Questions worth asking

Before making an enquiry, decide what interests you: a general explanation, a particular number calculation or the significance attached to names and dates.

Ask for a clear explanation of the method, the details required and the limits of its claims. Only share personal details you are comfortable providing after the consultation scope is agreed.
