## What is a Kundli?

A *Kundli*, also called a Janam Kundli or birth chart, represents the positions of celestial bodies for a particular date, time and location according to an astrological calculation. In Vedic astrology, practitioners use the chart to discuss symbolic themes associated with life and personality.

Think of a Kundli as a **framework for interpretation**, not as a scientific measurement of character or future events. Different astrologers may emphasize different aspects of the same chart, and the conclusions are not guaranteed facts.

## The role of birth information

A traditional birth chart often uses a person's date of birth, birth time and birthplace. The time and location affect some calculations, including the ascendant and house positions.

If the exact time is unknown, it is better to explain that uncertainty than to invent one. Ask the astrologer which parts of an analysis remain possible and what limitations apply. Share sensitive personal information only when it is necessary for the consultation.

## Signs, planets and houses

A few recurring words are useful when reading about Vedic astrology:

- **Signs** are twelve divisions of the zodiac used as a symbolic reference system.
- **Planets** are interpreted according to rules within the tradition. The astrological use of the term does not always match the astronomical definition.
- **Houses** represent different life topics within the chart's interpretive structure.
- **Dashas and transits** are traditional ways to discuss astrological periods and movements.

These terms describe a belief-based interpretive system. They do not establish that a chart can predict events with scientific reliability.
